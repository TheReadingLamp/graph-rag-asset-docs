# SF6 Circuit Breaker Gas Handling Specification, Version 1

Effective 2024-06-15. Applies to all 33 kV SF6 feeder breakers.

## 2. Operating limits
2.1 Minimum SF6 operating pressure: 0.65 MPa at 20 C.
2.2 Maximum permissible leak rate: 0.5 percent per year.

## 5. Refill procedure
5.1 Refill only with gas conforming to IEC 60376; record density before and after refill.
