# Transformer Oil and Bushing Maintenance Standard, Version 3

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to all 132/33 kV power transformers at Riverside substation.

## 3. Acceptance limits
3.1 Minimum oil dielectric strength: 45 kV (raised from 40 kV).

## 4. Test intervals
4.2 Bushing insulation test interval: 3 years (reduced from 6 years following repeated bushing failures).
4.3 Oil change interval: 10 years, or on condition.

This document supersedes spec_trf_oil_v2. Work performed after 2026-01-01 must comply with this version.
