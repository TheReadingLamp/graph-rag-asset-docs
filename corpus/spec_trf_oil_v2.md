# Transformer Oil and Bushing Maintenance Standard, Version 2

Effective 2025-01-01. Applies to all 132/33 kV power transformers at Riverside substation.

## 3. Acceptance limits
3.1 Minimum oil dielectric strength: 40 kV.

## 4. Test intervals
4.2 Bushing insulation test interval: 6 years.
4.3 Oil change interval: 12 years, or on condition.
